Everything you need to know about ordering, delivery and pickup at Common Room.

Browse the menu and tap Add on the meals you want. Open your cart, tap Checkout, fill in your name, phone number and whether you want delivery or pickup, then tap Send order on WhatsApp. Your order opens in WhatsApp ready to send.

Yes. Common Room delivers to selected hostels around Egerton University, with priority for CBD and nearby hostels. Choose Delivery at checkout and we'll confirm whether your hostel is covered and any delivery fee. Not sure? Ask us on WhatsApp first.

When you select Delivery, the order form asks for your hostel name, building or block, room number and any extra instructions (for example “call me at the gate”). These details are included automatically in your WhatsApp message.

Your order starts when you press send in WhatsApp. Common Room then replies to confirm your meals, delivery arrangements and any delivery fee. Your order is only confirmed once we reply.

The menu shows our regular meals, but availability can change during the day. We confirm on WhatsApp, and if something is sold out we'll suggest an alternative. For a quick check, message us before you order.
