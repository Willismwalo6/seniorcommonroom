Our story

A seat for every student. And every lecturer.

Common Room sits right here at Egerton University — a restaurant for the whole campus. Students between lectures, lecturers between classes, anyone who simply wants a proper meal without the fuss.

It started with a simple thought: a good meal shouldn't cost you half your break standing in a queue. So we built Common Room around your time — real Kenyan meals at fair prices, and an online order you can send in a minute from wherever you are.

Order ahead on your phone, then collect when it's ready or ask for hostel delivery. Less waiting, more eating, and a break that's actually yours.
