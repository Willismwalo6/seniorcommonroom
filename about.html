About us

YOUR CAMPUS FOOD SPOT.

Common Room is a restaurant at Egerton University for students and lecturers alike — good food, fair prices and an easier way to eat on campus.

Our story

A table for every student

Common Room was built around a simple idea: campus food should be delicious, affordable and easy to get. We cook the Kenyan meals students love — from a quick ndazi and chai to a full plate of chicken or fish.

And because lecture timetables don't wait for long queues, we made ordering simple: browse, order on your phone, then collect or have it brought to your hostel.
