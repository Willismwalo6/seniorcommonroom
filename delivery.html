Your hostel details

Help us find you

The clearer your details, the faster your food reaches you. When you pick Delivery, the order form asks for:

  • Hostel name — the hostel you stay in.
  • Building or block — if your hostel has more than one.
  • Room number — so we know exactly where to go.
  • Phone number & notes — so we can reach you, plus any instructions (e.g. “call at the gate”).

Order confirmation

You're never left guessing

Sending your order on WhatsApp starts the conversation — it isn't final until Common Room replies. We'll confirm that your meals are available, whether we can deliver to your hostel, and any delivery fee. If something is unavailable, we'll suggest an alternative.
